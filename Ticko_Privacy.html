Ticko Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ticko, a smartwatch Pong game developed for HarmonyOS-based wearable devices. This Privacy Policy explains how Ticko handles information in connection with your use of the app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

Ticko is designed to run entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share:

  • Personal identification data (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Health or biometric data from your smartwatch sensors
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Game scores or session history

2.2 Local Device Data

Ticko stores only the following data locally on your device, which never leaves it:

  • Selected difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard)

This data is used exclusively to provide in-game functionality and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. Device Permissions

Ticko requests the following device permissions solely to provide core gameplay features:

  • Vibration: used to provide haptic feedback when a goal is scored. No vibration data is recorded or transmitted.
  • Audio output: used to play in-game sound effects stored locally within the app package. No microphone access is required or requested.

No permission is used to collect, monitor, or transmit any information about you or your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Ticko does not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or cloud services. No data is shared with or sold to any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

Ticko does not collect any personal information from users of any age. The app is safe for use by children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and since we collect no data at all, there is no risk of inadvertent collection.

6. Data Security

Because Ticko does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal data at risk of breach, unauthorized access, or misuse. All app functionality operates offline and entirely within your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable regulations. Any changes will be posted within the app and will indicate the updated effective date at the top of this document.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
