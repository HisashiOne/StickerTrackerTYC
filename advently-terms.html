01 About Advently

Advently is a Christmas Advent calendar application developed for Huawei HarmonyOS wearable devices. From December 1 to December 24 it reveals one gift per day: a Christmas recipe, a tip for connecting with your loved ones, or a fact about Christmas traditions around the world. Content is available in English, Spanish, Italian, French and Chinese.

These Terms and Conditions explain the rules for using Advently and how the app handles information. Advently does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information such as:

  • Personal identification data (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Health or biometric data from your smartwatch sensors
  • Payment information

The App is free and does not require account registration or login.

02 Content for Information Only

All content in Advently (recipes, tips and Christmas facts) is provided for entertainment and general information purposes only. It does not constitute professional, medical, nutritional, psychological, or any other kind of advice.

03 Recipes, Allergies and Safety

Some gifts are simple recipes. Before preparing any of them, please:

  • Check every ingredient for possible allergies or intolerances. Some recipes contain eggs, milk, nuts or gluten.
  • Take care when handling hot liquids, stoves and ovens.
  • Supervise children at all times in the kitchen.

You prepare and consume any recipe at your own risk.

04 Local Data Storage

Advently stores only the following information locally on your device:

  • Which calendar doors you have already opened during the current season

This data remains exclusively on your smartwatch and is used only to show your opened gifts. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it resets automatically each Christmas season.

05 No Internet Connection

Advently works completely offline. All content is included in the app, and the app does not make any network requests. No information of any kind leaves your device.

06 Date and Time

Calendar doors unlock according to the date and time set on your smartwatch. If your device's date is incorrect, doors may unlock earlier or later than expected.

07 Data Sharing, Analytics and Tracking

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to third parties. Advently does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or user profiling tools. Your usage of the App is not monitored, recorded, or reported in any form.

The App does not integrate with social networks, cloud storage providers, or advertising platforms.

08 Children's Privacy

Advently is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users. Since no personal data is collected at all, there is no risk of inadvertent collection from users of any age group. Children should prepare recipes only with adult supervision.

09 Data Control

Because the only stored information stays on your device, you keep full control over it. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your smartwatch.
