01 About Futbalia

Futbalia is a smartwatch application developed for Huawei HarmonyOS wearable devices. It provides football statistics, standings, and team data for the global football championship. This Privacy Policy explains how Futbalia handles information in connection with your use of the app.

Futbalia does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information such as:

  • Personal identification data (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Health or biometric data from your smartwatch sensors
  • Payment information

The App does not require account registration or login.

02 Local Data Storage

Futbalia may store the following preferences locally on your device:

  • Favorite teams and display preferences
  • App configuration and settings

This data remains exclusively on your device and is used only to provide core app functionality. It is never accessible to us or any third party.

03 Internet Connectivity

Futbalia requires an internet connection to fetch up-to-date football statistics, standings, and team data from third-party sports data providers. No personal data is transmitted during these requests.

All network requests are read-only data fetches and contain no personally identifiable information.

04 Third-Party Data Sources

The sports data displayed in Futbalia is sourced from third-party sports data APIs. We do not control the content of these external sources and are not affiliated with any official football governing body or tournament organizer.

Access to these data sources is governed by their respective terms of service and privacy policies.

05 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to third parties. API requests made by the App contain no personally identifiable information.

06 Analytics and Tracking

Futbalia does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or user profiling tools. Your usage of the App is not monitored, recorded, or reported in any form.

07 Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with social networks, cloud storage providers, or advertising platforms. Futbalia is designed exclusively for Huawei smartwatches running HarmonyOS and operates within that environment only.

08 Children's Privacy

Futbalia is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users. Since no personal data is collected at all, there is no risk of inadvertent collection from users of any age group.

09 Data Control

Because your preferences are stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data. We recommend using your device's built-in security features to protect your information. Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data from your device.
